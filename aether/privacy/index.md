## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how FlowTech Computing (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information when you use Aether (the “App”), a mobile application for airline crew members. By creating an account or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

FlowTech Computing is a sole proprietorship based in Canada. We are the controller of personal information processed through Aether.

If you have questions about this policy or your personal information, contact us at flowtechcomputing@gmail.com.

## 2. Who This Policy Applies To

Aether is designed for airline crew members (pilots and flight attendants). The App is not intended for, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from, anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 3. Information We Collect

### 3.1 Account and Authentication Information

When you create an account, we collect:

- Email address (for login and account recovery)
- Display name (chosen by you)
- Profile photo URL (if you sign in with Google or Apple)
- Authentication provider (email/password, Google Sign-In, or Apple Sign-In)
- A unique account identifier assigned by Firebase Authentication

If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your email address, name, and (for Google) profile photo from those providers. We do not store the underlying OAuth tokens - those are managed by Firebase Authentication. Apple users may choose to share a private relay email address; we honor that choice.

### 3.2 Employment and Profile Information

To provide schedule features, we collect work-related information:

- Airline (employer code)
- Employee ID
- Home base (airport code)
- Department (e.g., Flight Operations, In-Flight)
- Rank (within department)
- Aircraft qualifications
- Administrator or moderator status (if applicable)

You may also optionally provide:

- Full legal name
- Profile image (uploaded by you)

### 3.3 Schedule and Work Data

The core function of Aether is displaying your work schedule. We process:

- Monthly schedules (block hours, credit hours, year-to-date totals, days off)
- Trip and pairing details (duty days, crew assignments, status)
- Flight segment information (departure/arrival airports and times, gates, aircraft, OOOI timestamps)
- Hotel and layover information (hotel name, phone, city, shuttle details)
- Bid package data (provided by your airline or by you)

### 3.4 Social and Friend Data

If you use friend features, we process:

- Friend connections (other Aether users you have approved as friends)
- Friend requests (sent and received)
- Schedule visibility settings

When two users become friends, both can view each other’s schedules within Aether.

### 3.5 Messages and Chat

If you use chat features, we process:

- Message contents
- Sender and recipient identifiers
- Timestamps (sent, edited, deleted)
- Reactions and replies

Chat messages are visible to the participants of the conversation only.

### 3.6 Device and Push Notification Data

To deliver notifications, we collect:

- Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) push tokens
- Firebase Installations device identifier
- Token expiration timestamps

We do not collect advertising identifiers, device hardware identifiers, or device fingerprints.

### 3.7 Analytics, Diagnostic, and Crash Information

We use Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics to understand how the App is used and to improve reliability.

Firebase Crashlytics processes crash and error data, including:

- Crash stack traces
- Device model and operating system version
- Application context at the time of the crash

Firebase Analytics processes app-usage data, including:

- Screen views and navigation paths within the App
- Session duration and frequency
- App version, operating system version, and device model
- General region (country level), inferred from IP address; we do not store the IP address
- Custom events we record to understand feature use (for example, which schedule views or actions are most used)

We use this data in aggregate to identify reliability issues, prioritize improvements, and understand which features are valuable to users. We do not use Analytics data for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it to third parties. We do not collect advertising identifiers (IDFA on iOS or AAID on Android).

### 3.8 Feedback and Bug Reports

If you submit feedback or a bug report, we collect the contents of your submission, the platform you submitted from, and your account identifier.

### 3.9 Content Moderation and Audit Logs

For users with administrative or moderation roles, and for actions that affect shared data, we maintain:

- Audit logs (event, actor, resource, before/after changes, timestamp)
- Pending edits (proposed changes awaiting review)
- Violation history (for users whose contributions have been flagged)

### 3.10 What We Do Not Collect

Aether does not collect:

- Device location (GPS or network-based)
- Contacts
- Photos or media (except a profile image you choose to upload)
- Call logs, SMS, or browsing history
- Health, fitness, or financial data
- Advertising identifiers

The App does not request location permissions on your device.

### 3.11 On-Device Settings (Not Transmitted)

Your in-app preferences - theme, dark mode, time format, temperature unit, notification toggles, schedule filters, layout choices - are stored only on your device and are not transmitted to our servers.

## 4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Authenticate you and protect your account
- Display your work schedule, trips, flights, and related information
- Enable friend connections and shared schedule visibility
- Deliver chat messages between you and other users
- Send push notifications you have opted into (friend requests, messages, schedule updates)
- Diagnose crashes and improve App reliability
- Understand feature usage in aggregate to prioritize improvements
- Respond to your feedback and bug reports
- Detect, investigate, and prevent abuse, content violations, and security incidents
- Comply with legal obligations

We do not use your personal information for advertising. We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Where applicable law (such as the EU/UK GDPR) requires us to identify a legal basis for processing, we rely on:

| Purpose | Legal Basis |
| --- | --- |
| Providing the App’s core scheduling features | Performance of a contract with you |
| Sending operational notifications | Performance of a contract; consent (where required) |
| Friend connections and shared schedules | Consent |

## 6. How We Share Your Information

We share personal information only as described below.

### 6.1 With Other Aether Users

- Friends: Users you approve as friends can see your display name, profile image, employment context, and schedule.
- Crew members on shared flights or trips: Other crew assigned to the same flight or trip may see crew assignment information (name, rank, employee ID) within the App.
- Chat participants: Anyone in a conversation with you can see your messages and display name.

### 6.2 With Service Providers (Sub-Processors)

We use the following third-party services to operate the App. These providers process personal information on our behalf under contractual safeguards.

- Google LLC (Firebase) - provides authentication, database, file storage, server functions, push notifications, and crash reporting. Processes account, profile, schedule, chat, push tokens, and crash data.
- Apple Inc. - provides Sign in with Apple. Receives your email and name during sign-in only.
- Google LLC (Google Sign-In) - provides Google Sign-In. Receives your email, name, and profile photo URL during sign-in only.

We also use the following services server-side only (your device does not contact them directly) to enrich schedule and flight data:

- FlightAware - flight tracking data (flight number, airports)
- Google Places API - hotel name and phone lookup
- NOAA Aviation Weather - airport weather (no personal data sent)
- Visual Crossing - civilian weather data (no personal data sent)

### 6.3 With Your Airline

Schedule, trip, flight, and crew data within Aether is scoped to your airline. Other crew members of your airline may see information necessary for crew coordination (such as crew assignments on a shared flight).

### 6.4 For Legal Reasons

We may disclose information when we reasonably believe disclosure is required to comply with a legal obligation, court order, or lawful government request, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of FlowTech Computing, our users, or others.

### 6.5 Business Transfers

If FlowTech Computing is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your personal information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you and provide choices where required by law.

### 6.6 We Do Not Sell Your Information

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for cross-context behavioral advertising. This is true regardless of jurisdiction, including the meanings given to “sale” and “sharing” under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA).

## 7. International Data Transfers

FlowTech Computing operates from Canada. Our service providers, including Google (Firebase), may store and process data in the United States or other countries.

For users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland: when we transfer personal information outside your region, we rely on safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses, the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (where applicable), and adequacy decisions (Canada has been recognized as providing adequate protection by the European Commission).

For users in Canada: under the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), we use contractual measures to ensure that personal information processed by service providers outside Canada receives a comparable level of protection. By using the App, you acknowledge that your information may be processed outside the country in which you reside, and may be subject to lawful access requests in those jurisdictions.

## 9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have some or all of the following rights:

- Access - request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
- Correction - ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information
- Deletion - ask us to delete your personal information
- Portability - receive your information in a structured, machine-readable format
- Restriction or objection - limit or object to certain processing
- Withdraw consent - where we rely on consent
- Lodge a complaint - with your local data protection authority

### 9.1 Canadian residents (PIPEDA)

You have the right to access your personal information and to challenge its accuracy. You may also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada at https://www.priv.gc.ca.

### 9.2 EU/EEA, UK, and Swiss residents (GDPR / UK GDPR)

You have the rights listed above and may complain to your local supervisory authority. We do not have an EU representative at this time given the scale of the App; if this changes we will update this policy.

### 9.3 California residents (CCPA / CPRA)

You have the right to know, delete, correct, and limit certain processing. You also have the right not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. You may designate an authorized agent to make a request on your behalf.

### 9.4 How to exercise your rights

Email flowtechcomputing@gmail.com with the words “Privacy Request” in the subject line and tell us what you would like to do. We may need to verify your identity by confirming details associated with your account. We will respond within the time required by applicable law (typically 30 days under PIPEDA and GDPR, 45 days under CCPA, with extensions where permitted).

### 9.5 Account deletion

You can request deletion of your Aether account and associated personal information at any time. Detailed instructions and a request form are published in our Account Deletion notice, which is linked from our App Store and Google Play listings. To start the process directly, email flowtechcomputing@gmail.com with the subject “Account Deletion Request.” An in-app account deletion option is also planned for a future release.

When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize associated personal information from Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Realtime Database, and Cloud Storage. Some information may persist in encrypted backups for a limited period before being overwritten in the ordinary course, and we may retain limited information where required by law or for legitimate purposes such as resolving disputes or enforcing our agreements.

## 10. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including:

- Encryption in transit (TLS) for all communication between the App and our servers
- Authentication via Firebase Authentication (including OAuth flows for Google and Apple)
- Access controls through Firestore and Realtime Database security rules
- Server-side enforcement of permissions via Firebase Functions
- Audit logging for sensitive administrative actions

No security measure is perfect. We will notify you and applicable regulators of a data breach where required by law.

## 11. Children

Aether is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe we have collected information from a minor, contact flowtechcomputing@gmail.com and we will delete it.
